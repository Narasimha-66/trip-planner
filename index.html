Agra

Agra is a wonderful destination for people who love history, architecture and culture. It is home to the world-famous Taj Mahal, which attracts millions of visitors every year. Visitors can explore beautiful monuments, enjoy delicious local food and experience India's rich historical heritage.

    Famous attractions:
           1. Taj Mahal: A magnificent white marble monument built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.
           2. Agra Fort: A historical fort known for its beautiful architecture and Mughal history.
           3. Fatehpur Sikri: A historic city famous for its grand Mughal buildings.
           4. Mehtab Bagh: A beautiful garden offering a wonderful view of the Taj Mahal.
    Best time to visit: October to March.
    Famous food: Petha, Bedai and Jalebi.
    Interesting fact: The Taj Mahal is one of the Seven Wonders of the World and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Goa-The Beach Paradise of India

Goa is a perfect destination for people who love beaches, adventure and relaxation. With its beautiful coastline, exciting water sports, colourful markets and delicious food, Goa offers an unforgettable holiday experience.

    Famous attractions:
            1. Baga Beach: Famous for beach activities, nightlife and water sports.
            2. Calangute Beach: One of the most popular beaches in Goa.
            3. Fort Aguada: A historic Portuguese fort with beautiful sea views.
            4. Basilica of Bom Jesus: A famous UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its historical importance.
            5. Dudhsagar Falls: A spectacular waterfall surrounded by greenery.
        Best time to visit: November to February.

        Famous food: Goan Fish Curry, Bebinca and Prawn Balchão.
        
        Interesting fact: Goa was under Portuguese rule for around 450 years before becoming part of India in 1961.

Mysore - The city of Palaces

Mysore is a wonderful destination for people who love history, culture and architecture. From the magnificent Mysore Palace to the beautiful Brindavan Gardens, the city offers many attractions. Visitors can also enjoy delicious local food and experience the famous Dasara celebrations. 

        Famous attractions:
                1. Mysore Palace: A magnificent royal palace known for its beautiful architecture and lighting.
                2. Chamundi Hills: A famous hill destination with the Chamundeshwari Temple.
                3. Brindavan Gardens: A beautiful garden famous for its musical fountain.
                4. St. Philomena's Church: One of the tallest churches in India, known for its Gothic architecture.
                5. Mysore Zoo: A popular zoological park with a variety of animals and birds.
                Best time to visit: October to February.

            Famous food: Mysore Masala Dosa, Mysore Pak and Mysore Bonda.

            Interesting fact: Mysore is famous for its world-famous Dasara festival, which is celebrated with grand processions and cultural events.

Araku Valley - The Paradise of Andhra Pradesh

Araku Valley is a wonderful destination surrounded by green hills, beautiful waterfalls and coffee plantations. Visitors can explore natural caves, experience tribal culture, enjoy scenic train journeys and taste the famous Araku coffee. It is an ideal place for a peaceful holiday.

        Famous attractions:
                1. Borra Caves: Famous limestone caves with beautiful natural rock formations.
                2. Katiki Waterfalls: A beautiful waterfall surrounded by green forests.
                3. Coffee Museum: A place where visitors can learn about the history and production of Araku coffee.
                4. Tribal Museum: Showcases the lifestyle, traditions and culture of local tribal communities.
                5. Chaparai Waterfalls: A scenic destination surrounded by natural beauty.
            Best time to visit: October to March.

            Famous food: Bamboo Chicken, Araku Coffee and local tribal cuisine.

            Interesting fact: Araku Valley is famous for its organic coffee, which is grown in the Eastern Ghats.

Manali – The Mountain Paradise

Manali is a beautiful hill station surrounded by snow-covered mountains, green valleys and flowing rivers. It offers exciting adventure activities, peaceful natural views and delicious local food. It is a perfect destination for people who love mountains, nature and adventure

        Famous attractions:
                1. Solang Valley: Famous for adventure activities like paragliding, skiing and zorbing.
                2. Rohtang Pass: A beautiful mountain pass known for its snow-covered landscapes.
                3. Hadimba Temple: An ancient wooden temple surrounded by beautiful cedar forests.
                4. Old Manali: Famous for its peaceful atmosphere, cafes and beautiful streets.
                5. Jogini Waterfalls: A scenic waterfall surrounded by mountains and greenery.
    Best time to visit: March to June for pleasant weather; December to February for snowfall experiences.

            Famous food: Siddu, Dham and Madra.

            Interesting fact: Manali is located at an altitude of approximately 2,050 metres above sea level.
